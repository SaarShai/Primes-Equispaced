# Bottom line

For weight-2 rank-0 elliptic-curve newform f, the Conrey-Snaith ratios polynomial coefficient a_2(f) is

  **a_2(f)/a_4 = 12 − 12·B(f) + 6·B(f)² + 6·κ_2(f)**

with B(f) the verified a_3 arithmetic data (γ_E + H_unram + S_mult + S_add) and

  **κ_2(f) = (3/4)·[(L''/L)(1, sym²f) − ((L'/L)(1, sym²f))²]**
            **− (1/2)·S_mult^(2)(N) − (1/4)·S_add^(2)(N) − log(2π)**

where the bad-prime second-cumulant sums are
  S_mult^(2)(N) = Σ_{p‖N} [(log p)² · p/(p+1)² + 2(log p)² · (1/p)/(1+1/p)]
  S_add^(2)(N)  = Σ_{p²|N} [(log p)²/(1−1/p)² + (log p)² · (1/p)²/(1−1/p²)²]
