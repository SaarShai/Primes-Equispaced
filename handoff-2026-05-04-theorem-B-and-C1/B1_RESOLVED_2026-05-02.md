# B1 — Closed-form per-curve L'/L(1, sym²f), validated

## Bottom line

The wrap-doc mechanism (W2_C1_FINAL_WRAP §4) holds with **exact** per-curve `(L'/L)_anal(1, sym²f)` values from pari/gp. 16-curve fit MAE = 0.1347, R²_no-int = 0.9489, 221a1 residual +0.079. Comparable to the wrap's MAE 0.1014 with truncated values. **The 0.734 / arithmetic-correction story is validated.**

## Normalization

pari `lfunsympow(E, 2)` uses **arithmetic** norm (Dirichlet coeffs `a_p² − p`, FE `s ↔ 3-s`). Our paper uses **analytic** (`λ_p² − 1`, FE `s ↔ 1-s`). Translation:

  L_arith(s, sym²f) = L_anal(s − 1, sym²f)

(integer shift: a_p² − p = p(λ_p² − 1) at good primes). Therefore

  **(L'/L)_anal(1, sym²f) = (L'/L)_arith(2, sym²f)**

— i.e., evaluate pari's derivative at arith s = 2, not s = 1. Verified Opus 4.7 derivation.
