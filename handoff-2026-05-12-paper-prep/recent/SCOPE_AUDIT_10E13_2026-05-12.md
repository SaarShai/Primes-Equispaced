# Scope audit — `10^{13}` in our internal record vs Koyama's paraphrase

## TL;DR

We have rigorous internal evidence at the `~1.3 · 10^{13}` scale, **but
only for the Dominance-of-`-1` prime-residue-count replication**. We
have **no internal `K = 10^{13}` verification of any analytic
NDC/Dirichlet/EC/C_1/B_∞ claim**. The two scales must not be conflated
in the manuscript.

## Where Koyama's 2026-05-12 paraphrase comes from

In his 2026-05-12 email, Koyama writes:

> "your recent updates on the `e^{-γ}` factor, the conductor-confounded
> trend, and the requirements for the shifted Perron remainder are
> fascinating. I believe these 'open challenges' and the **rigorous
> verification you've done at `10^{13}`** should be the core of our
> paper."

He is collapsing two strands that occurred concurrently in his inbox:

1. **The analytic strand:** corrected `e^{-γ}` factor, conductor-
   confounded trend, shifted Perron remainder — all at the `K ≤ 10^7`
   analytic scale.
2. **The Dominance-of-`-1` Phase-1 strand:** "two independent
   implementations agree to `1.3 · 10^{13}`" — at `x = 1.3·10^{13}`
   for prime-residue counts.

These two strands are *not* the same computation. Koyama's paraphrase
elides this distinction. We should restore it.

## Manuscript wording — what we will print, in scope

The honest wording for the section, broken into two distinct
subsections:

**(A) Phase-1 Dominance-of-`-1` replication.**

> "Phase-1 replication of Koyama's Dominance-of-`-1` residue-count
> tables (Koyama, *nontriv.pdf*) was carried out through
> `x = 1.3·10^{13}` with two independent implementations agreeing on
> every residue count at the headline scale for moduli
> `q ∈ {7, 8, 11, 19, 23}`. A second hardware path independently agrees
> through `x = 1.3·10^{12}`. Identity (3.1) of the source is verified
> across 495 cells. The dominance signal is reproduced for
> `N ∈ {7, 8, 19}`. A subset of table cells (`N = 11`, `N = 19`, Table 4
> entries) shows unresolved discrepancies; their reconciliation is
> deferred to a forthcoming joint note."

**(B) Analytic verification of the corrected `B_∞`, `C_1`, and
AK-normalization claims.**

> "Independent of the residue-count replication, the analytic
> identities of §X were verified numerically at the following scales:
> the corrected `B_∞` identity across the four `(χ, ρ)` pairs at
> `K = 2·10^6` with 50-digit precision (residuals from `3·10^{-5}` to
> `3·10^{-3}`, consistent with the predicted `K^{-1/2}/\log K`
> conditional-tail decay); the subleading constant
> `C_1 = -L''(\rho)/(2L'(\rho)^2)` at the same `(χ, ρ)` pairs (matched
> to 6+ decimals against an independent re-derivation); the
> Dirichlet drift `\mathrm{mean}|D_K| \cdot \zeta(2)` from `0.992` at
> `K = 2·10^6` to `0.974` at `K = 10^7`, consistent with the
> `e^{-\gamma}` (Aoki–Koyama) target and incompatible with `1/\zeta(2)`
> at the `1/\log K` scale."

The two scales (`x = 1.3·10^{13}` for residue counts, `K = 10^7` for
analytic identities) appear in *separate* subsections; neither is
allowed to inherit the other's scale.

## Verdict

The user audit is **CORRECT**. The manuscript must not, in any form,
state "NDC / Dirichlet / EC / `C_1` / `B_∞` verified up to
`K = 10^{13}`." It must state "Phase-1 residue-count replication at
`x = 1.3·10^{13}`" separately from "analytic identities verified at
`K ≤ 10^7`."
